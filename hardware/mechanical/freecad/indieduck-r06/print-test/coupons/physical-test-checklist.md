# IndieDuck R06 first-fit record

Draft physical checklist, 08-10-2026. All items below remain unverified. The trial requires five Feetech HD-1910 servos with their horns and compatible fasteners, the specified 10 × 15 × 3 mm ankle bearing, M2 trial screws, measured nominal Ø3 mm locator pins and the printed R06 left-leg parts. Availability and exact hardware variants have not been confirmed.

## Print record

- [ ] Record source revision, STL filenames, slicer version and saved 3MF/job filename.
- [ ] Confirm P2S, installed 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and selected PLA profile. Record the actual PLA product, colour and spool condition.
- [ ] Print coupons flat, with bearing recesses facing upward. Record walls, infill, temperatures, cooling, bed plate, brim/support choice and any XY/hole or elephant-foot compensation actually used. Nominal CAD has no compensation.
- [ ] Let the coupons cool fully before removing and measuring. Record lifting, cracks, rough bores or blocked holes. Preserve the unmodified coupon for the first comparison; separately record any deburring or drilling.
- [ ] Identify the large upper-left corner key, row notches and column notches before testing. Photograph the oriented coupons with their IDs visible in the record.

## Coupon fit checks

- [ ] Measure the plate thickness and overall X/Y dimensions with available measuring tools. Record the instrument and resolution; do not infer precise internal diameters from an unsuitable tool.
- [ ] Try an actual M2 screw in each pilot from the top, by hand tool, with clearance below for the tip. Record starting effort, cracking, stripping, seating and removal. Do not reuse a stripped hole as fresh-fit evidence. A through-hole coupon does not prove adequate blind-hole depth or final screw length.
- [ ] Try an actual M2 screw through each clearance hole without cutting a thread. Record free passage and visible play.
- [ ] Test each locator socket with an actual measured nominal Ø3 mm pin intended for the skate interface. Record its type and measured size. Record a drill-shank substitution separately; the CAD pin envelope is not a selected hardware specification.
- [ ] Measure and identify the actual ankle bearing. Trial the three bearing seats from the open face, supporting the plate and loading only the outer ring. Record free/drop fit, hand-push fit, refusal to enter, rocking, seated depth and damage. Stop if force would damage the bearing or coupon. Do not hammer it into place.
- [ ] Record preferred pilot, clearance, locator and bearing variants as observations. Retain the printed samples. Propose any robot CAD changes separately for review and a new revision.

## Actual left-leg assembly checks

- [ ] Keep power disconnected for the initial mechanical trial. Identify and record the five actual servos for hip yaw, hip roll, hip pitch, knee and ankle, their horns and screws. Confirm each actual envelope and mounting pattern against the intended R06 pocket before fastening.
- [ ] Check servo insertion and removal with the horn fitted, cable-exit clearance, connector access, rear protrusion clearance and mounting-hole alignment. Record each pocket separately.
- [ ] Confirm screw type and length from the available inventory. Check actual engagement, tip clearance and access with neighbouring servos installed; a nominal M2 designation alone does not establish compatibility.
- [ ] Record the assembly sequence used for the left-leg trial; a complete illustrated assembly manual remains open. Confirm horn seating, recess fit, shaft-screw access, visible gaps and retained fasteners. Do not force a misaligned joint into place.
- [ ] Trial the actual ankle bearing and printed boss in the actual shank/foot. Record fit, full seating, retention, rotation and play; the bearing coupon tests only its outer-seat fit.
- [ ] Inspect the assembled rounded foot and separate TPU 95A sole. Print TPU as a separate external-spool job. Record sole attachment, flush seating, removal effort and any locator/latch damage. Do not combine the PLA and TPU profiles into one unverified job.
- [ ] Check permitted mechanical motion while avoiding forced servo back-driving. Record cable pinching, collisions, fastener access and joint play through the documented safe travel. Powered motion requires the project's separately checked electrical setup and limits.
- [ ] Perform and record repeated disassembly/reassembly appropriate to the workshop trial, noting worn pilot holes, cracked layers, locator/latch wear and lost retention. Record the actual number of cycles and observations; do not infer durability from a single assembly.

## Non-rolling skate interface trial

- [ ] Remove supports from the separate retaining key and inspect its thin edges and screw passage.
- [ ] Use the deck fixture with two measured nominal Ø3 mm pins, 37 mm centres, to trial the R06 foot sockets and key pocket. Confirm fit, full seating and extraction without forcing the parts.
- [ ] Trial a correctly sized M2 key-retention screw by hand and record pilot engagement, tip clearance, access and removal. The cropped deck does not test wheel forks, axle/bearing retention, rolling clearance or load.

## Record the boundary of the result

- [ ] Photograph the actual completed left-leg assembly and any interference, with part revision and orientation identifiable.
- [ ] Record pass/fail/open findings by part and exact interface. Separate as-printed results from any hand-fitted results.
- [ ] Keep load capacity, walking, skating, impact resistance, get-up/roll behaviour and policy transfer marked unverified until their separate tests are performed. A fitted coupon or assembled leg does not establish those outcomes.
