Mirrored parts inherit their source credits. Hidden construction geometry and older independent source bodies are retained for editability. No XGO derivative geometry or Pollen meshes are imported. Full native geometry and export checks are in `../validation/`.

## Public asset inventory

[R06-source-map.json](R06-source-map.json) maps all 33 selected printed objects, the two main native files, three native coupon documents, matching exports, and oriented print meshes to their source relationships and SHA-256 hashes. Prior study files are identified by hash and are not bundled. [source-register.csv](source-register.csv) is filtered to sources actually used for these CAD adaptations, numerical component references, printing facts and the body comparison.

The public skate fixture has corrected Apache-2.0 and creator metadata. Every non-null shape was compared before and after this metadata-only change; see `../validation/publication-metadata-checks.json`. The main CAD pair and neutral exports are unchanged copies of R06.

Configured 3MF projects, vendor profiles and manufacturer G-code templates remain outside this repository. Our own plain settings and a normalized factual slice summary are provided. No original MicroDuck or XGO geometry is distributed. The body proportion measurements are numerical evidence for future work; current geometry is unchanged.
