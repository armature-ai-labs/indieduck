# Workshop build format

Workshops accommodate up to 15 participants, working in teams of three on up to five robots. Each team assembles and trains one robot. Teams may purchase their robot; otherwise the lab retains, disassembles and reuses it.

Use repeatable subassembly checkpoints and retain each team's fit observations. Start with unpowered inspection and assembly, then locally simulated control and RL workflows. Powered commissioning and training transfer follow hardware validation; no trained behaviours are bundled with R25.

Workshop duration, final fees, robot purchase prices and assessed outcomes are not fixed by this release. Do not present planned equipment or job outcomes as guarantees.
